We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something is not right, here is how cancellations, returns and refunds work.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order from My Orders any time before it ships. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.

Returning an item

For delivered orders, open the order page and use the Return request button. To be eligible:

  • The request must be made within 7 days of delivery.
  • The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

Refunds

  • Refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method within 5-7 working days.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are made by bank transfer.

Non-returnable items

Some items cannot be returned for hygiene or safety reasons, such as personal care products and opened consumables. Any such restriction is mentioned on the product page.

Need help?

If you have a question about a return or refund, please reach out via our contact page.
