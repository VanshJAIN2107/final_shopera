Program rules

The fine print, in plain words

How is my commission calculated?
Commission is earned on the order total after discounts, at the program rate of {{ rate|floatformat:"-2" }}%.
When does a commission become approved?
A commission starts as pending and becomes approved when the order is delivered. Only approved commissions can be withdrawn.
When is a commission cancelled?
If the order is cancelled or refunded, the commission on it is cancelled too.
Can I earn on my own purchases?
No. Orders placed from your own account do not earn commission.
Who gets the credit if a customer clicks several links?
The last affiliate link clicked within {{ cookie_days }} days before the order gets the credit.
How do payouts work?
Payouts are manual. Once your approved balance reaches ₹{{ min_payout }}, request a payout from your dashboard and we send it to your saved UPI id or bank account.
What gets an account suspended?
Spam, fake clicks or orders, and misleading claims about {{ site_name }} or its products lead to suspension, and unpaid commissions may be cancelled.
